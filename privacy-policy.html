Monologs Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit Monologs.

Information we collect

We may collect basic information such as your email address if you join the waitlist, plus usage data from cookies and site analytics.

How we use it

We use the information to improve the experience, communicate updates, and keep the site running smoothly.

Your rights

You have the right to unsubscribe from our email updates at any time. To unsubscribe, email us at monologs@gmail.com. If you request it, we will also delete your personal information from our systems so you can be forgotten.

Emails collected through the waitlist form are processed by StaticForms. Monologs itself does not directly store those email submissions on its own servers.

Cookie choices

You can manage your cookie settings through the consent banner. Necessary cookies are always enabled, while analytics and advertising cookies can be disabled.
