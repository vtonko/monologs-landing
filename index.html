Monologs is a film frame logger that notes exactly when you take a photo. Then, it writes this data to your scans so you don't lose track of them.

How it works

When you take a photo, Monologs notes exactly when and where you took it.

When you develop and scan your film, it tags the photos with their time and location of capture.

Your timeline now shows your film photos on the exact day you took them.
